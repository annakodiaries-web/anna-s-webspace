Free creator portfolio template: UGC and PR media kit, no sign-up

This is a free, in-browser template for building a UGC, beauty or lifestyle creator portfolio. The page above is a sample you can edit. Pick a style, colours, fonts and stickers, rewrite the text, add your photos, then download your own one-file website or save it as an image to send to brands. Template by Annako (@dear_annako).

How it works

  1. Tap any text or photo on the sample page to edit it.
  2. Use the bar at the bottom to change the style, colours, fonts and stickers.
  3. Press Save to download your website (index.html) or a PNG, then put it online free with Netlify Drop.

Questions

Is this creator portfolio template really free?

Yes. It is free to use, there is no sign-up and no account, and the website you download is yours to keep.

Do I need to code?

No. You edit the page directly in your browser: tap any text to rewrite it, tap a photo to swap it, and add or remove sections like Get to know me, quick numbers, brands, videos, services, rate card and contact.

Can I change the colours, fonts and style?

Yes. Choose from 7 style concepts (Clean, Clipboard, Lace & polka, Gingham diary, Newspaper, Coquette and Storybook), 13 colour palettes or your own custom colours, 10 font styles, 9 background patterns and 8 photo frames. You can also drag stickers such as embroidered patches, washi tape, binder clips, buttons, chrome, wax seals, fabric corners and labels anywhere on the page.

How do I put my portfolio online?

Press Save, then Download my website to get a single index.html file. Put it in a folder, go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag the folder onto the page for a free link. You can also save the page as a long PNG image to send to brands.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, the editor and the portfolio you make are built to work on a phone. Editing on a computer is easier for uploading photos and for the Netlify Drop step.

Where are my edits stored?

Your edits are saved in your own browser on your device, not on a server. Download your website when you are done so you have a copy, because clearing your browser data can erase the saved draft.

Who made this template?

It was made by Annako (@dear_annako), a UGC and beauty creator. Exported sites keep a small "template by @dear_annako" credit link at the bottom.
